# Verification round 2: European mass casualty incidents (unresolved and conflicting figures)

Compiled 30 October 2026. Every figure below comes from a page opened in this session (URL given). Figures taken only from news outlets are labelled **news only**. Where nothing could be opened, the entry says **not found**. Quotations are given in the original language with a translation.

Confidence: **High** = official government, parliamentary, court or public-health document, or peer-reviewed paper, stating the figure directly. **Medium** = a single such source with a caveat (early snapshot, internal inconsistency, hearing testimony rather than a published report), or two official sources that differ. **Low** = news only, or still unresolved.

Access notes. The proxy refused direct downloads (curl) from assemblee-nationale.fr, dekamer.be, sintef.no and dserver.bundestag.de, and WebFetch truncates long PDFs at roughly 80,000 to 95,000 characters, so the body of the French inquiry report (r3922-t1, 2 volumes), the Bundestag report (19/30800), the Belgian inquiry's first interim report (DOC 54 1752/006, chapter 5 "Victimes" at pp. 92-98) and the NOU 2012:14 health chapters could only be read in part. The French inquiry's **hearing transcripts** (comptes rendus) were short enough to read in full and are used instead. pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov returned a CAPTCHA page; interieur.gouv.fr, letemps.ch and diarimes.com returned client errors; web.archive.org is blocked from this session.

## Item 1. Paris, 13 November 2015: injured totals 352 / 351 / 495 / "more than 300"

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Verdict** | **Resolved: the figures count different things, and none is wrong.** The French state's own victim list (LUV) gives **130 dead, 493 physically injured, 1,124 psychologically injured ("choquées")**, total 1,747 (June 2016). The "352/351" figures are the early physical-casualty count used by clinicians; "413" is AP-HP's "blessés hospitalisés"; "495" is the LUV physical-injury figure as of early 2017 (one more than 493). |
| **Findings and what each counts** | (a) **Government victim list, June 2016.** Juliette Méadel, secrétaire d'État chargée de l'aide aux victimes, to the Assemblée nationale commission d'enquête, 16 June 2016: « on compte 1 747 victimes sur la dernière LUV [liste unique des victimes], dont 130 décédées, 493 blessées et 1 124 choquées » ("the latest single victim list counts 1,747 victims: 130 dead, 493 injured and 1,124 shocked", the last being psychological casualties without physical injury). Same hearing: « 151 personnes ont perdu la vie sur le sol français en raison d'attentats – 130 le 13 novembre ». (b) **Ministry of Justice** (page on the trial): « les six attaques qui ont provoqué le décès de 130 personnes et fait plus de 350 blessés » ("130 dead and more than 350 injured"). (c) **AP-HP** (press page, Nov 2016): « Les attentats du 13 novembre 2015 ont fait 130 morts et 413 blessés hospitalisés » ("130 dead and 413 injured people admitted to hospital"). (d) **SAMU de Paris (Prof Pierre Carli), inquiry hearing 16 March 2016:** « 130 morts » and « 350 blessés »; Pitié-Salpêtrière « n'a reçu que cinquante blessés, soit 15 % du total ». **BSPP (Paris Fire Brigade), same hearing:** « 100 urgences absolues et 157 urgences relatives » (257 prehospital-triaged casualties), with Dr Tourtier later citing « 98 urgences absolues »; the record does not reconcile 100 vs 98. (e) **Service de santé des armées, hearing 24 Nov 2015 (Défense committee):** « sur les 98 [urgences absolues] qui ont été dénombrées dans les hôpitaux franciliens »; the two military hospitals (Percy, Bégin) took « 52 blessés … dont dix-huit urgences absolues », « treize d'entre eux étant admis en réanimation », « Cinquante interventions chirurgicales ont été réalisées en quarante-huit heures ». (f) **Santé publique France** (research page): « 130 morts, près de 700 blessés et plusieurs centaines de personnes exposés » (the "près de 700" bundles physical and psychological injury; its news page says only « des centaines de blessés »). (g) **Inquiry rapporteur, 5 July 2016:** « le taux de mortalité hospitalier était très faible – environ 1,5 % des blessés » and « il n'y a eu aucun transfert inter-hospitalier ». (h) **Dr Patrick Pelloux, hearing 14 March 2016:** « les 129 autres sont mortes sur les lieux des attentats » and « une seule victime est morte après son transfert à l'hôpital » (which conflicts with Hirsch's 4 in-hospital deaths and with M. Hirsch's testimony of « deux à leur arrivée, une le 14 novembre au matin »; the sources do not reconcile this). (i) **JEMS (Ernouf, Tourtier et al., BSPP authors; trade journal):** "With 130 dead and 352 wounded" and 59 victims at Stade de France, 120 at the terrace sites, 114 surviving victims at the Bataclan. **Why the figures differ:** 350/352 is the physical-casualty count circulated in the first days and used in the clinical papers; 413 is AP-HP's count of casualties admitted to hospital region-wide (it includes people admitted later and at non-AP-HP sites); 493/495 is the state's legal count of physically injured victims on the LUV, which kept growing as victims came forward; 1,747 and "près de 700" add psychological casualties. |

## Item 5. Oslo and Utøya, 22 July 2011: 69 and 60 vs 68 and 61

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Verdict** | **Resolved by the 22 July Commission (NOU 2012:14): 8 killed in the government quarter and 69 killed at Utøya (77 in all).** The O-TC paper's "68 deaths at scene" plus "61 injured" is consistent with one person who was alive when evacuated and died later; the Commission counts that person among the 69 killed. The Commission does not give a single "injured" total for Utøya; it gives 33 with life-threatening or serious physical injury. |
| **Findings** | NOU 2012:14, ch. 1: « 77 mennesker ble drept » ("77 people were killed"). Ch. 2.1 (government quarter): « Åtte mennesker ble drept momentant og ti innlagt på sykehus med livstruende eller alvorlige skader » ("eight people were killed instantly and ten admitted to hospital with life-threatening or serious injuries"). Ch. 2.2: « I alt ti hardt skadde ble sendt direkte til traumesenteret på Ullevål » and 190 patients with less serious injuries were treated at the Oslo emergency clinic (Legevakten) in the first 24 hours. Ch. 2.1 (Utøya): « Til sammen 69 mennesker, de fleste ungdommer, ble drept i massakren » ("in all 69 people, most of them young people, were killed in the massacre"). Ch. 2.3: « I tillegg ble ytterligere 33 personer påført livstruende eller alvorlig fysisk skade » ("a further 33 people suffered life-threatening or serious physical injury"). The Norwegian government's English page: "a bomb exploded in the Government Office Complex in the center of Oslo killing eight people" and "69 people were killed in a mass shooting on Utøya island". The Commission text read does not state explicitly that one Utøya victim died after evacuation; the later chapters (hospital response) were beyond the part of the PDF that could be read. |
| **Source URLs** | https://www.sintef.no/globalassets/project/nexus/nou201220120014000dddpdfs.pdf (NOU 2012:14, first ~80,000 characters, chapters 1-2) ; https://www.regjeringen.no/en/aktuelt/22-july-terror-attack/id2979790/ . |
| **Confidence** | **High** for 8 + 69 = 77 dead; **High** for 10 hospitalised with serious injury from the bomb and 190 minor injuries at Legevakten; **High** for 33 seriously injured at Utøya; the 60/61 "injured" totals in the papers remain paper-level figures (**Medium**), and the one-later-death explanation is an inference (**Medium**). |
| **Suggested wording** | "The 22 July Commission's official count is 77 dead: 8 in the Oslo government quarter and 69 at Utøya. Ten bomb casualties were admitted with serious injuries and about 190 minor injuries were treated at the Oslo emergency clinic. At Utøya, 33 people suffered life-threatening or serious physical injury; clinical papers give about 60 injured in total. Papers giving 68 dead at Utøya and 61 injured count one casualty who died after evacuation as injured rather than dead." |
