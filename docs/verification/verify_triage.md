## Correct TST algorithm as published

Published by NHS England / NARU, version 1.0, April 2023. The NARU supplementary guidance, NHS England tool PDF and long-read give the steps in this order:

1. **WALKING?** Yes → **P3**. "People who are uninjured should be directed to a relative place of safety"; injured walking casualties go to a Casualty Collection Point (CCP).
2. **SEVERE BLEEDING?** Yes → **Pressure / Tourniquet / Packing** → **P1**.
3. **TALKING?** ("the response check is simplified to 'are they Talking?' with yes or no").
   - Yes → **PENETRATING INJURY (front or back)?** This is "a deliberate targeted search for penetrating injuries to the 'torso'", which "must include the neck, chest, armpits, back, abdomen, groin and buttocks, (front back and sides)". Any such injury → **P1**. None → **P2**.
   - No → go to the breathing step.
4. **BREATHING?** "Open airway if able", then "a 10-second breathing check" (Look, Listen and Feel).
   - Yes → **Place in recovery position** → **P1**.
   - No → **Place in recovery position** → **NOT BREATHING**, with "**CPR if resources allow**". Resuscitation "must not detract from more important LSIs of stopping external bleeding and opening an airway."
5. Additional rule: "any child under 2 should be automatically triaged as a P1."

**Bands:** P1 white on red; P2 black on yellow; P3 white on green; Not Breathing black on **silver**. "A white checked border is used to identify the TST bands", which separates them from MITT triage cards.

**No DEAD category:** "TST does not have a DEAD category unlike other triage systems." The stated reason is that Not Breathing is "used to simply illustrate the objective finding of a breathing check." Later, "a healthcare practitioner with appropriate time reassesses a casualty with a silver 'Not Breathing' [band] and considers them a viable resuscitation (P1) or ROLE/PLE (DEAD)." The NHS England letter of 18 April 2023 says: "the tool does not triage casualties to dead but labels as not breathing."

**MITT (secondary triage, NHS responders):** Catastrophic bleeding → P1. Walking → P3. Breathing? If not, open the airway; still not breathing → DEAD (in children under 12, consider 5 rescue breaths after submersion, immersion or smoke inhalation). Responds to voice? If not → recovery position, P1. Aged under 2 → P1. Breathing rate outside 12–23 → P1. Heart rate 100 or more → P1. Otherwise → P2. MITT **keeps a DEAD category**. It contains **no "Pit Stop" pathway**.
