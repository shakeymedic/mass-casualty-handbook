# Verification round 2: UK incident claims (30 October 2026)

Rules applied: a claim is "confirmed" only where a source opened in this session states it. Official inquiry/inquest/sentencing documents, peer-reviewed papers and NHS/government/trust statements are primary; BBC/ITV/PA-syndicated copies are acceptable for headline facts and are labelled "news only". Nothing is filled from memory. Where a page could not be opened, the attempt is recorded.

Blocked this session: standard.co.uk (direct, web.archive.org and archive.ph copies all "SITE_BLOCKED"); southport.public-inquiry.uk (robots); judiciary.uk judge-led-inquests index (robots); Springer full text (paywall, no open-access copy per Semantic Scholar); BMJ j4309 full text (paywall). gov.uk and assets.publishing.service.gov.uk refuse direct curl via the proxy; WebFetch reads only the first ~120,000 characters of each PDF/HTML, which for the Southport Inquiry Volume 1 ends inside Chapter 2, well before the NWAS section (page 142 per the contents table).

Note on claim 1: the brief labels the Croydon University Hospital (CUH) claims "London Bridge/Borough Market 2017". They belong to the **Croydon tram derailment, 9 November 2016** (06:07 crash; CUH took 31–38 casualties). CUH was not a receiving hospital for London Bridge 2017 (NHS England lists King's, Royal London, St Thomas', UCH and St Mary's). The site should correct the attribution wherever it appears.

## Summary

Resolved (confirmed, partly confirmed or corrected with a source opened this session): 48/48 survival (LAS); London Bridge receiving hospitals (NHS England, re-confirmed); Southport stab-wound counts (sentencing remarks); Southport NWAS/HART/MERIT response (NWAS statement plus NWAS evidence to the Inquiry); Southport receiving hospitals (NWAS); Southport survivability claim (contradicted by Inquiry expert evidence as cited by NWAS); Finsbury Park hospitals (NHS England, corrects the site); Fishmongers' Hall place of death and pre-hospital response (PFD report, LAS); Forbury Gardens place of death for Furlong (Record of Inquest) and survivors' hospitals (news only); 12.1% PPV meaning (Borsetto abstract); CUH "validated 2023 triage" (unsupported).

Still not found: all five CUH tram-crash operational details (ED emptied in 20 min, WhatsApp, coach of 29, 07:30, upgrades on arrival); London Bridge van speed; King's patients before the 23:04 declaration (topic exists in BMJ j4309 but figures are behind the paywall); 90% penetrating at King's; Manchester operations up to 14 hours; Fishmongers' Hall receiving hospitals by name; Wails and Ritchie-Bennett Records of Inquest; hospital in which Alice da Silva Aguiar died; the five Zone 2 / exploration percentages; the Southport Inquiry Volume 1 Chapter 4 text itself (NWAS section, page 142) and the exact wording of Recommendations 9–10.
