# Fact-check: incident details (10 incidents)

Checked against: the incident table (report lines 36–281) and the narrative (lines 282–456). Verdicts are strict. "Confirmed" means I read a source that states the fact. Several outlets were blocked to the fetch tool (Guardian, Evening Standard, Independent, Manchester Evening News, Croydon Advertiser, web.archive.org), so some claims that rest only on those are marked Could not verify.

## Internal inconsistencies noted
1. **Manchester:** the narrative says 22 victims = 20 at the scene + 3 in ED, which sums to 23. The source figure of 20 includes the bomber, so 19 victims died at the scene.
2. **Manchester:** 59 ambulance-transported + 95 self-presented = 154, against a cohort of 153. This comes from the EMJ paper and is not flagged in the report.
3. **London Bridge:** the table and narrative call all 12 Royal London and all 14 King's patients "critical", which gives 26. That exceeds the 21 critical across all 5 hospitals. The true critical figures were 6 and 8.
4. **Croydon:** the table says ~50 treated at CUH, but about 51 was the total across CUH and St George's together (31 at CUH and 20 at St George's on LAS figures). The narrative's "two-section" tram contradicts RAIB's three-section description.
5. **Southport:** "100% survival of those reaching surgery" alongside one child dying in hospital the next day.
